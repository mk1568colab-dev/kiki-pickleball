WHY PICKLEBALL?

A familiar product.
A demanding business.

Kiki uses the paddle market to connect competing product designs, changing customer preferences, supplier choices, and technology shifts. Students must decide not only what to sell, but whether their operations can support it.

The teaching focus is not finding one winning formula. It is explaining why a strategy works in one market setting and struggles in another.

A FEW PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Before the first round.

Do students need to install anything?

No. Students access the hosted game through a browser and sign in to the classroom assigned by their instructor.

Is Kiki a marketing or an operations game?

Teams make pricing and product choices, but the teaching focus is operations and supply chain management: materials, capacity, quality, inventory, service, and cash determine whether a plan can be executed.

Can I use it with more than one class?

The application supports separate classrooms. Each section competes within its own game. Classroom access and instructor permissions should be arranged before teaching.

Can I try a live game from this page?

This website contains demonstration screenshots and introductory resources. Live classroom access is arranged separately; this page does not accept game sign-ins or connect to active classrooms.

Do I need the source code to teach with Kiki?

No. The intended instructor experience is to use an assigned hosted classroom and teaching resources. Application source code and student records are not distributed through this page.
